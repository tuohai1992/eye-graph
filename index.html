02Geography

03Instrument use

Cell counts show reported values; shading shows the share within each condition group. Direct elicitation dominates the older retina and glaucoma literature, while population surveys rely on EQ-5D.

04Severity gradient

Utility falls as disease or vision loss worsens, across conditions and instruments.

Each line is one study following one instrument from its least to its most severe state. In AMD, TTO utility falls from 0.89 with good better-eye vision to 0.40 at counting fingers or worse; thyroid eye disease vignettes start low even in the mildest state.

05Method matters

Rows are studies that valued the same patients with more than one method. HUI3 tends to sit lowest because it captures vision directly; standard gamble against blindness sits highest because few patients will risk sight.

How this graph was built

Record flow

Method

The workflow follows EQ-Graph by Abundance Decision Systems, with the funded-project anchor replaced by a topic search. Europe PMC was searched for any eye condition (about 70 condition and vision terms, from glaucoma and AMD to uveitis, thyroid eye disease and visual impairment), combined with a utility term block (utility, EQ-5D, time trade-off, standard gamble, HUI, SF-6D, CHU-9D, AQoL, QALY).

Rules removed letters, editorials and similar article types, records that are not about an eye condition, and records whose title and abstract contain no utility-type number. The remaining 177 abstracts were screened one at a time and tagged to one of 16 condition groups. A study was included when its abstract reports a preference-based utility value, decrement or gain for people with the condition.

Every extracted value and sample size was checked by machine against the abstract text; all 304 checks matched, with eight sample sizes written as words in the abstract.

Graph model

Nodes: study, condition, health state, instrument (with method class), country. Edges: a study addresses a condition, reports values for health states, uses instruments, and is set in a country.

Limits

Values come from abstracts only, so subgroup values reported only in full-text tables are missing. Screening and extraction were done by Claude without a second human reviewer. Four values are derived and marked. Vignette, carer, clinician and student valuations are labelled by respondent type. Two myopia papers report the same sample of 2,198 adolescents and the same CHU-9D mean, so they likely share one cohort. Cost-effectiveness models and reviews were set aside for a later phase.
